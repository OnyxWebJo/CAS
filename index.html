HOW WE WORK

From signal to response.

A seamless 4-stage operational protocol executed within seconds of any security trigger.

01 Signal Transmission

System Connection

Your premises alarm and sensors connect securely to the CAS central monitoring matrix via encrypted dual-path telemetry.

02 24/7/365 Watch

Continuous Monitoring

Certified security controllers monitor incoming telemetry streams 24 hours a day, 365 days a year without interruption.

03 Human Intelligence

Instant Verification

Alerts are immediately verified through live telemetry, eliminating false alarms and identifying genuine emergencies in seconds.

04 Coordinated Action

Rapid Response

CAS executes agreed escalation protocols, notifying owners, deploying ground teams, and coordinating direct official emergency dispatch.
